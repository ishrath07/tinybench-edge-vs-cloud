Tokens/watt lands within a narrow 3.2–3.9 band across all three edge platforms — power efficiency barely differentiates them. The deployment decision is really about hardware capex and thermal endurance, not watts: the Galaxy S24 Ultra couldn't produce a valid sustained-rate data point at all — without active cooling, it isn't a viable always-on inference platform, full stop. That's a sharper deployment verdict than any efficiency number on this chart.

RTX 4050 & Hailo-10H figures: TinyBench, measured. iPhone 16 Pro power: estimated from third-party sustained-load chip benchmarks, flagged. Cloud rate: RTX 4060-class, nearest live-rentable proxy (RTX 4050 is laptop-only, not a datacenter SKU). Electricity: BESCOM Bengaluru domestic tariff, ~$0.074/kWh. All figures converted at ₹95/USD.
